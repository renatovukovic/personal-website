This paper introduces a post-training framework for large language models that leverages reinforcement learning from self-generated feedback from answer confidence in logical reasoning tasks, reducing reliance on human annotations. The approach demonstrates that models can iteratively improve alignment and performance by learning from their own evaluations, offering a scalable alternative to traditional RLHF ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.21931)).
