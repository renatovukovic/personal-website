This paper analyzes the geometric structure of contextual language model representations, showing that token-level embeddings often lie on low-dimensional local manifolds despite high ambient dimensionality. Using tools from geometric and topological data analysis, it reveals systematic layer- and context-dependent patterns in intrinsic dimensionality, offering new insights into representation efficiency and organization ([GitHub](https://github.com/aidos-lab/Topo_LLM_public), [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.01034)).
